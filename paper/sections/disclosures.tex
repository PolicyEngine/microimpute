\subsection*{Data and code availability}
\begin{sloppypar}
The \texttt{microimpute} package is open-source and available at \url{https://github.com/PolicyEngine/microimpute}. The 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances is publicly available from the Federal Reserve Board (\url{https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm}). Current Population Survey data are publicly available from the U.S. Census Bureau (\url{https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps.html}). The Enhanced CPS 2024 dataset used in this study is produced by PolicyEngine and available through the \texttt{policyengine-us-data} package (\url{https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-us-data}). The Jupyter notebook that reproduces all results presented in this paper is available in the repository at \url{https://github.com/PolicyEngine/microimpute/blob/main/paper/imputing-from-scf-to-cps.ipynb}.
\end{sloppypar}
